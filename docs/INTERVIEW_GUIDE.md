# Interview Prep Guide

A reference version of Week 3's interactive interview question bank. See `content/week3/interview-questions.json`, `talking-points.json`, and `je-dunn-culture.json` for the machine-readable sources, and `#week3` on the site for the interactive version.

## How to Use This Guide

1. Read each question and the "why they're asking" note — understand the intent before memorizing an answer.
2. Practice out loud, not just in your head. Use the Mock Interview Simulator (Week 4) to time yourself.
3. Anchor every answer in a specific, real example wherever possible.

## Opening Questions

- **Tell me about yourself.** (90-120 sec) — Lead with your background, then bridge directly into why construction and why this role.
- **Why are you transitioning to construction?**
- **Why JE Dunn?** — Be specific: employee-owned, century-old, South Central region, Meta El Paso scale.
- **What do you know about the Meta El Paso project?**
- **How does your Army background apply here?**

## Technical FE Questions

- **Walk me through a quality inspection — what are you looking for?**
- **What's the difference between an RFI and a submittal?**
- **How would you handle a defect you find during an inspection?**
- **Describe your experience with construction schedules.**
- **What tools do you use to document site progress?**

## Behavioral / Situational

- **Tell me about a time you had to coordinate multiple teams.** → Use your Project #8 story.
- **Describe a time you caught a mistake before it became a problem.** → Use your Project #11 story.
- **What would you do if a subcontractor was non-compliant?**
- **Tell me about your experience with documentation and compliance.**

## Questions to Ask Your Interviewer

- What does success look like in this FE role at JE Dunn?
- Can you describe the team I'd be working with?
- What are the biggest challenges Field Engineers face on this project?
- What tools/software does JE Dunn use for project management?
- What's the typical career path from Field Engineer to Superintendent?

## Talking Points

**Your story:** "I've managed phased operations with hard deadlines and zero-margin compliance. Construction is the same logic applied to trades and quality gates."

**Why you're good at this:**
- Leadership experience running teams of 16+ people under pressure
- Documentation discipline built from years of compliance-critical reporting
- A risk-mitigation mindset — scanning for what could go wrong before it does
- Comfort operating in high-stakes, zero-margin-for-error environments

## JE Dunn Culture Snapshot

- Founded 1924, 5,001-10,000 employees, 100% employee-owned
- 8th largest general contractor in the U.S.
- Founding philosophy: "Treat people fairly, and offer quality service at a fair price."
- South Central region: Dallas, Austin, Houston, Oklahoma City, El Paso
- Common interview themes: safety, quality, teamwork, integrity, continuous improvement
