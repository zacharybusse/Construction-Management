# Full Terminology Reference

A print/reference version of the interactive glossary at `#glossary` on the site. See `content/week1/terminology.json` for the machine-readable source.

## Structural

**Rebar** — Reinforcing steel bars embedded in concrete to provide tensile strength.
*Why it matters:* You verify spacing, cover, and tie placement to ensure structural integrity before the pour.
*Army analog:* Like ammunition supply verification — correct quantities, placement, and documentation before the mission starts.

**Concrete Pour** — The process of placing liquid concrete into formwork to cure into a structural element.
*Why it matters:* A critical inspection point — affects structural integrity, schedule, and budget if it goes wrong.

**Formwork** — Temporary molds used to shape and hold concrete in place until it cures.
*Why it matters:* You verify dimensions, plumb, and bracing before the pour — errors here are locked into the structure permanently.

**Structural Steel** — Steel beams, columns, and connections that form a building's structural skeleton.
*Why it matters:* You verify bolt torque, weld quality, and plumb/alignment against structural drawings.

**Plumb and Level** — Plumb means perfectly vertical; level means perfectly horizontal.
*Why it matters:* Out-of-tolerance elements compound as construction progresses.

**Footing** — The base structural element that transfers building loads into the soil.

**Layout** — Physically marking building elements' exact locations on site using survey equipment, based on drawings.

**Curing** — Maintaining moisture and temperature in concrete after pouring so it gains strength properly.

**Cover (Concrete Cover)** — The distance between reinforcing steel and the outer surface of the concrete.

## Documentation

**RFI (Request for Information)** — A formal question submitted to the design team when drawings are unclear, conflicting, or missing information.

**Submittal** — Documentation (product data, shop drawings, samples) a subcontractor submits for architect/engineer approval before fabrication or installation.

**Change Order** — A formal, agreed-upon modification to the contract scope, cost, or schedule.

**Punch List** — A list of incomplete or defective items that must be corrected before a phase or project is accepted as complete.

**Daily Report** — A formal daily record of site activity: weather, manpower, work performed, deliveries, and issues.

**As-Built Drawings** — Drawings updated to reflect the actual final installed conditions.

**Shop Drawing** — Detailed fabrication drawings produced by a subcontractor or supplier.

## MEP

**MEP** — Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing: the three primary building systems trades.

**Rough-In** — The stage where MEP systems (pipes, conduit, ductwork) are installed inside walls/ceilings before they're closed up.

**MEP Coordination** — Resolving physical clashes between mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems before installation.

**HVAC** — Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning.

**Switchgear** — Electrical equipment used to control, protect, and isolate electrical equipment.

## Scheduling

**Critical Path** — The sequence of dependent tasks that determines the shortest possible project duration.

**Look-Ahead Schedule** — A short-term (2-3 week) detailed schedule breaking the master schedule into actionable near-term tasks.

**Milestone** — A significant, fixed point in the schedule marking completion of a major phase.

**Float** — The amount of time a task can be delayed without impacting the overall project schedule.

**Mobilization** — The setup phase where a contractor establishes site infrastructure before construction begins.

**General Requirements** — The contract section covering project-wide items: site logistics, safety, temporary facilities, administration.

## Quality

**QA/QC** — Quality Assurance / Quality Control: verifying work meets specifications before and after installation.

**Inspection Checklist** — A standardized checklist used to verify specific installation criteria before work is covered.

**Spec Compliance** — Verifying installed work matches project specifications.

**Non-Conformance Report (NCR)** — A formal document identifying work that does not meet specifications.

**Safety Stand-Down** — An immediate, mandatory work stoppage to address an identified safety hazard.
